Solution 2 — Pipeline Integrity & Operational Risk Intelligence

Overview:
Pipeline integrity is critical to safe and reliable energy operations. Building on Solution 1 — Enterprise HSE Performance Management, this solution shifts the focus from workforce safety to the integrity and operational risks associated with pipeline infrastructure.
Using the PHMSA Hazardous Liquid Accident dataset, the solution transforms historical pipeline incident data into actionable risk intelligence to identify high-risk assets, understand failure patterns, assess incident consequences, and prioritise integrity management efforts.

Business Question:
How can historical pipeline incident data be used to identify operational risk, understand why incidents occur, and prioritise pipeline assets for integrity management?

Key Analytics:
* Pipeline incident trends
* Geographic risk assessment
* Asset and infrastructure risk
* Failure mechanisms and leak characteristics
* Financial, environmental, and human impact
* Integrity risk prioritisation
* Strategic risk intelligence

Business Value:
The solution helps pipeline operators understand where risk is concentrated, how failures occur, and what consequences they create, supporting more targeted inspection, maintenance, resource allocation, and proactive pipeline integrity management.

Technology:
SQL | Microsoft Excel |  Power BI | MS Word

Data Source:
PHMSA Hazardous Liquid Accident Dataset

Project Type:
Enterprise Analytics | Pipeline Integrity | Operational Risk | Asset Risk | HSE Analytics


Data Disclaimer: Keystra Energy Group is a fictitious organisation created for this portfolio. All HSE data are synthetic and generated specifically for this project. This solution is a proof of concept and has not been deployed or tested within a real company. Findings should not be interpreted as actual company performance.
